Quick start

This article describes how to connect XenMan to a pool and what to do after the first sign-in.

Add a pool

To add a pool:

  1. Go to Settings > Pools and select Add Host.
  2. Enter a name, the address of the pool coordinator, the port (443) and the credentials of a pool administrator, typically root.
  3. Select Save. XenMan shows the SHA-256 fingerprint of the coordinator's certificate.
  4. Compare the fingerprint with the value on the host, then trust the certificate. On the host, run:
    openssl x509 -noout -fingerprint -sha256 -in /etc/xensource/xapi-ssl.pem

XenMan learns the other hosts of the pool and their certificates over this verified connection. The pool appears in the navigation on the left.

Important: Do not trust a fingerprint you have not compared. XenMan connects only to hosts that present a trusted certificate.

After the first sign-in

We recommend the following steps:

  • Mail – configure an SMTP server under Settings > Mail & Logging for notifications and password resets.
  • Users – create accounts for your team under Users and assign roles and pools. See Users and roles.
  • Two-factor sign-in – turn it on for your account under Settings > Account.
  • Certificate – replace the self-signed certificate under Settings > Appliance > HTTPS Certificate. See HTTPS certificate.
